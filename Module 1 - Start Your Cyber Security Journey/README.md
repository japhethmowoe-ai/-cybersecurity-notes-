# Module 1 – Start Your Cyber Security Journey

## 🧠 Things I Need to Remember

- CIA Triad = Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability.
- Blue Team defends. Red Team attacks ethically.
- Phishing targets people more than computers.
- Malware is malicious software; ransomware locks data for payment.

## Core Concepts

### CIA Triad

| Part | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Confidentiality | Only authorized people access data. |
| Integrity | Data stays accurate and unchanged. |
| Availability | Systems stay accessible when needed. |

### Blue Team vs Red Team

| Blue Team | Red Team |
|-----------|----------|
| Detect attacks | Find vulnerabilities |
| Monitor logs | Penetration testing |
| Respond to incidents | Simulate attacks ethically |

### Common Threats

- Phishing
- Malware
- Ransomware
- Brute-force attacks
- Social engineering

---

## 🎯 Interview Questions

**1. What is the CIA Triad?**

Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability—the three core principles of information security.

**2. What's the difference between Blue Team and Red Team?**

Blue Team defends systems by detecting and responding to threats, while Red Team ethically attacks systems to find weaknesses.

**3. What is phishing?**

A social engineering attack that tricks people into revealing sensitive information like passwords.
